# Independent annotation guide

Review each fictional conversation without system predictions. Choose `scam`, `benign`, or `ambiguous` based on the script alone. Mark uncertainty and give a short rationale. Do not try to guess the product's rules or copy author labels. Legitimate urgency and synthetic assistive speech do not imply fraud. A quoted warning is not automatically an actionable instruction.

Use a pseudonymous annotator ID. Do not enter names, phone numbers, medical information, account details or victim stories. Record relevant conflicts: involvement in authoring cases, access to predictions, involvement in tuning rules. Return the annotation template to a custodian who does not tune against held-out labels. Independent source status may only be recorded after actual returned annotations exist.

If information is insufficient, mark `ambiguous`; do not invent facts. No audio is included, so do not make claims about speaker identity, cloning quality or real ASR accuracy.
